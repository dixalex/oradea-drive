THE GAME TODAY
- Oradea Drive: a free browser game (three.js, desktop now, phones planned) of the real city of Oradea, Romania. You drive a Cybertruck-style car that can also fly; once every sight is found a Roadster unlocks that leaps on cold gas. City traffic, pedestrians, trams, the river Crișul Repede, ~30 hand-built landmarks and churches (Piața Unirii, City Hall tower, the Moon Church, Queen Mary theatre, the fortress, the Baroque Palace and basilica, Calea Republicii's Secession fronts, the Black Eagle Palace, synagogues, Emanuel and BBSO Baptist churches, the Orthodox cathedral, the university and more). UI in Romanian and English. Car radio plays live local Oradea stations. It is a hobby fork of a friend's open-source game, Cherkasy Drive (Ukraine, AGPL-3.0). The owner runs a Romanian NGO, Drumul Vieții, that helps Ukrainian refugees and runs a kindergarten, so the tone must stay suitable for families and churches.
- Existing systems (data-driven, cheap to fill): (a) Missions: a dispatcher offers five types (courier, taxi, chases, muggings, and similar), with fines and money kept in the browser. (b) Explore quest: visiting every sight on the map unlocks the Roadster; sights light up on the map when found. (c) The quest book: the player picks one quest at a time and collects its cards by driving to them. A card is a hidden place with a story: the big map shows only a grey dashed search circle (offset from the spot), the book shows a hint, a faint light marks the place in the world; driving into it opens the story card with its source and pays a reward. Check the exact card format in src/game/quests/secrets.js. Cherkasy's quest «Таємниці Черкас» has 20 lesser-known places with stories. Oradea has no quests yet (src/game/quests/index.js: QUESTS = CITY ? CITY.quests ?? [] : [SECRETS]).
- Missing: any story, characters, progression beyond money and the Roadster unlock, social or sharing features.
