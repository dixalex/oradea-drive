Card: Oradea's road underpasses and overpasses (follow BRIEF.md for research order, budgets, verification and the report)

Goal: the passages Oradea built in recent years show in the game and can be driven: road underpasses with the surface
street on top, and road overpasses (over roads and the railway) on real decks instead of flat on the ground.

What the data shows (2026-10-22, tools/cherkasy/raw/oradea/roads.json):
- OSM has the underpasses as highway ways with tunnel=yes, and tools/cherkasy/build_map.mjs:209 drops every tunnel road, so
  none reach map.json: Pasajul Magheru, the ones at Piața Emanuil Gojdu, Piața 1 Decembrie, Strada Dimitrie Cantemir,
  Strada Tudor Vladimirescu (9 primary, 4 tertiary, 2 secondary, 1 trunk pieces), pedestrian Pasajul Vulturul Negru,
  ~19 footway tunnels. Pasajul Oneștilor and Pasajul Crișul are tagged highway=construction (check whether they opened).
- Road bridges over roads and the railway (Calea Aradului, Cantemir, Ogorului, Velența, the ring road...) are drawn on the
  terrain, so the car crosses the tracks at grade. Only the river bridges have decks (src/world/oradea/river.js).

Patterns to reuse from the Cherkasy engine (read them first): world/cherkasy/overpass.js (shapeOverpass: a cutting for
the lower road, a deck over it, deckAt), khimbridge.js and odeskabridge.js (viaduct decks, ramps, retaining walls),
railcut.js (a cut as its own height function that hf.heightAt takes the minimum with, ground triangles re-laid after
creation), bridgekit.js (shared helpers), npc/lanes.js opts.deckAt (traffic rides decks). An underpass is the new case: a
trench with portals and retaining walls, the lower road in it, the surface road kept on a deck or slab over the portal
span; lanes need both levels.

src/world/oradea/sites/ with SITE.shape for the terrain; a test per module; screenshots from the driver's seat going
through each passage. Ask before touching shared engine files beyond a guarded one-liner.
