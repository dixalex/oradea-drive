1. Casa Poynár, OSM way 6481083 (Piața Ferdinand 1 / Str. N. Grigorescu 6; tagged `house`, probably the whole corner plot: split it into the real building and its neighbours if it is). The corner tower with a bulbous cupola where Calea Republicii starts, semicircular top-floor windows, curved triangular pediments with round windows, butterfly motifs on the balconies, the raven relief on the square front. 1907–1911 for Ioan Poynár, Ferenc Sztarill (oradeaheritage.ro/casa-poynar/; ro.wikipedia says April–November 1907: give the range, not one year). Quest card 'poynar' (src/world/oradea/quests.js) stands at its door.
2. Palatul Füchsl, OSM way 1534824825 (Str. Mihai Viteazul). Sept 1902–April 1903 for the wine-merchant brothers, Zoltán Bálint and Lajos Jámbor; two storeys, the tower over a rounded corner, a wavy cornice, vine and grape ornament, the «FT» letters, kidney-shaped attic windows. The tram runs 15 m away: keep clear of it. Quest card 'fuchsl'.
3. Hotel Transilvania (opened 1904 as «Pannonia»), OSM way 421787916 (Str. Teatrului 2, the west side of the theatre square). Guttman József, 1903–1904; large arched ground-floor openings, the pastoral and floral relief frieze, paired top windows, floral cartouches. The hotel's name may show as it appears on the building today; no other brands.
